Every appliance is downstream of a decision. LevTech exists to make that decision measurable — before it becomes irreversible in a patient's mouth.

Digital Protocols

A protocol is a sequence that doesn't depend on who's having a good day. LevTech's case workflows standardize intake, imaging, landmarking and design review into a single repeatable path — the same rigor whether it's your first case of the morning or your last.

Built for teams, not individuals: any trained operator can pick up a case mid-workflow and know exactly what's already been decided, and what's still open.

3D Cephalometry

Traditional cephalometric analysis reads a 2D projection of a 3D skull — angles measured off a flattened shadow. LevTech's volumetric approach keeps every landmark in three dimensions, so measurements describe the actual anatomy, not its silhouette.

The result is a landmark set that stays accurate across head rotation and positioning error — the kind of small inconsistency that quietly compounds into a mis-planned case.

Alignment Mechanics

Tooth movement is a mechanics problem before it's a clinical one — force, resistance, anchorage. LevTech models the biomechanical plan first, then derives the appliance from it, rather than designing an appliance and hoping the mechanics cooperate.

Every movement in the plan is traceable back to a stated force and a stated reason — nothing moves "because the software suggested it."
