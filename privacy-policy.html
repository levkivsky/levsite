Who we are

This website is operated by Levkivsky, Ternopil, Ukraine ("Levkivsky", "we", "us"). For any privacy-related question, contact us at hello@levkivsky.com.

What data we collect

We only collect what you give us directly through the contact form on this site:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The message you write to us

We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies. We do not collect data automatically beyond standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp) which are used only for security and are not linked to your identity.

Why we collect it

We process this data solely to respond to your inquiry — a legitimate business interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, or, where you've ticked the consent box on our form, on the basis of your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Who we share it with

Your form submission is processed by Formspree, Inc., a third-party form-handling service based in the United States, solely to deliver your message to our inbox. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other third party for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

We retain contact form submissions for as long as necessary to respond to and resolve your inquiry, and no longer than 12 months unless a longer retention period is required to maintain a business relationship with you.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@levkivsky.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for dental professionals and business partners, not for children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest revision.
